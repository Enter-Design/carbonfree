CarbonFree has developed utility-scale projects with First Nations communities for more than fifteen years, beginning with Canada’s largest First Nations community on Grand Renewable Solar in 2013.

Our partnership structure is designed around the community’s interests rather than the developer’s convenience. Communities hold a controlling economic interest in the project partnership with governance representation, CarbonFree funds all development costs so no community capital is at risk, and the level and timing of any investment is the community’s to set.

All three projects awarded in the 2026 IESO LT2 procurement were developed in partnership with the communities whose territories host them.
